Why TypoFixr

Built for the moment when your words are mostly right and you just want the mistakes gone.

Fix text in place

TypoFixr replaces the text you selected instead of bouncing you through a separate editor.

Preserve tone and formatting

It focuses on obvious mistakes while keeping phrasing, lists, and paragraph structure intact.

Works from the menu bar

No Dock clutter. Trigger it with a shortcut and keep typing in the app you already use.

Undo-friendly

Corrections paste back like normal text changes, so standard undo works immediately.

Bring your own Groq key

Requests go directly from your Mac to Groq using your own account. No TypoFixr proxy or app account.

Security warnings

TypoFixr can warn about suspicious prompt text and sensitive data before anything is sent.

How it works

Three steps, no extra editor, no copy-paste gymnastics.

1

Select text

Highlight the text you want fixed in Notes, Mail, Slack, a browser, or another Mac app.

2

Press the shortcut

Use ⌘⇧D or your custom shortcut to send the selected text for correction.

3

Keep moving

TypoFixr pastes the corrected version back in place so you can continue writing immediately.

Requirements

  • macOS 13 Ventura or later
  • A Groq API key from console.groq.com
  • Internet access when running corrections
  • Accessibility permission so TypoFixr can replace selected text

Privacy

  • Your API key is stored locally in macOS Keychain.
  • Text is only accessed when you trigger a correction.
  • Correction history is stored locally on your Mac.
  • TypoFixr does not require an app account.
